# DyslexicBCP

Dyslexia-Friendly Book of Common Prayer and King James Bible

Using the Dyslexia-Friendly font [OpenDyslexic3](https://opendyslexic.org).

## Prayer Book

The prayer book is mostly from the 1662 BCP, with additional collects and prayers added from

- The 1928 Proposed BCP
- The Anglican Office Book (First Edition)
- The Book of English Collects
- Friends of Little Gidding
- And other sources

Inspired by [CatholicPenguin's Antiochian Western Rite BCP](https://github.com/CatholicPenguin/book-of-common-prayer).

Thanks to [Lynda M Howell's Online BCP](https://www.eskimo.com/~lhowell/bcp1662/index.html) for making the 1662 text easy to copy from.

## King James Bible

The text for the King James Bible comes from [Luke Smith's additions](https://github.com/LukeSmithxyz/kjv) to the [command-line KJV](https://github.com/layeh/kjv)
With corrections and edits based on the [Wikisource version of the King James Bible](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Bible_(King_James))
