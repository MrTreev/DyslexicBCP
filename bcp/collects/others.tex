\newcollect{Saints' Days}
\lett{A}{lmighty} God, who didst choose thine elect out of every nation, and dost shew forth thy glory in their lives: Grant, we pray thee, that following the example of thy servant Saint \changeable{N.}, we may be fruitful in good works to the praise of thy holy Name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Saint and Martyr}
\lett{A}{lmighty} God, by whose grace and power thy holy Martyr Saint \changeable{N.} triumphed over suffering and death: Endue us, we beseech thee, with the same power, that we may finish our course in faith, and with \changeable{him} receive the crown of everlasting life; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Martyr or Martyrs}
\lett{A}{lmighty} God, by whose grace and power thy holy Martyr \changeable{N.} triumphed over suffering and despised death: Grant, we beseech thee, that enduring hardness, and waxing valiant in fight, we may with the noble army of Martyrs receive the crown of everlasting life; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Doctor or Confessor}
\lett{O}{ God}, who hast enlightened thy Church by the teaching of thy servant \changeable{N.}: Enrich it evermore, we beseech thee, with thy heavenly grace, and raise up faithful witnesses who, by their life and doctrine, may set forth to all men the truth of thy salvation; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Bishop}
\lett{O}{ God}, the light of the faithful, and shepherd of souls, who didst set blessed \changeable{N.} to be a Bishop in the Church, that he might feed thy sheep by his word and guide them by his example: Grant us, we pray thee, to keep the faith which he taught, and to follow in his footsteps; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{An Abbot or Abbess}
\lett{O}{ God}, by whose grace the blessed \changeable{Abbot/Abbess N.}, enkindled with the fire of thy love, became a burning and a shining light in thy Church: Grant that we may be inflamed with the same spirit of discipline and love, and ever walk before thee as children of light; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Matron}
\lett{O}{ God}, who hast built up thy Church through the divers gifts and graces of thy saints: We give thee humble thanks for the example of holy women, and especially, this day, for thy servant \changeable{N.}; and we beseech thee to maintain among us the shelter of a mother's love and the protection of a mother's prayer; in the grace of thy Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Virgin or Virgin Martyr}
\lett{O}{ God}, who didst endue thy holy Virgin \changeable{N.} with grace to witness a good confession \changeable{and to suffer gladly for thy sake}: Grant that we, after her example, may be found ready when the Bridegroom cometh, and enter with him to the marriage feast; through the same thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{The Faithful Departed}
\lett{O}{ God} of the spirits of all flesh: We praise and magnify thy holy Name for all thy servants who have finished their course in thy faith and fear; for the blessed Virgin Mary, for thy holy Patriarchs, Prophets, Apostles, and Martyrs, and for all other thy righteous servants, known to us or unknown; and we beseech thee that, encouraged by their examples and strengthened by their fellowship, we also may be found meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light; through the merits of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{For Those Who Have Departed This Life}
\lett{O}{ Father} of all, we pray to thee for those whom we love, but see no longer. Grant them thy peace; let light perpetual shine upon them; and, in thy loving wisdom and almighty power work in them the good purpose of thy perfect will; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{Memorial Days}
\lett{A}{lmighty} God, our heavenly Father, in whose hands are the living and the dead: We give thee thanks for all those thy servants who have laid down their lives in the service of our country. Grant to them thy mercy, and the light of thy presence, that the good work which thou hast begun in them may be perfected through Jesus Christ thy Son our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{Synods of the Church}
\lett{A}{lmighty} and everlasting God, who by thy Holy Spirit didst preside in the Council of the blessed apostles, and hast promised, through thy Son Jesus Christ, to be with thy Church to the end of the world: We beseech thee to be present with the Synod now \changeable{about to be} assembled in thy Name. Save them from all error, ignorance, pride, and prejudice; and of thy great mercy vouchsafe so to direct, govern, and sanctify them in their deliberations by thy Holy Spirit, that through thy blessing the Gospel of Christ may be faithfully preached and obeyed, the order and discipline of thy Church maintained, and the kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ enlarged and extended. Grant this, we beseech thee, through the merits and mediation of the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{The Christianising of the World}
\lett{O}{ God} of all the nations of the earth: Remember the multitudes of the heathen, who, though created in thine image, are ignorant of thy love; and, according to the propitiation of thy Son Jesus Christ, grant that by the prayers and labours of thy holy Church they may be delivered from all superstition and unbelief, and brought to worship thee; through him whom thou hast sent to be our salvation, the Resurrection and the Life of all the faithful, the same thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{The Jews}
\lett{O}{ God}, the God of Abraham: Look upon thine everlasting covenant and cause the captivity of Judah and Israel to return. They are thy people, O be thou their Saviour; that all who love Jerusalem and mourn for her may rejoice with her; for Jesus Christ's sake, their Saviour and ours.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{Mohammedans and All Who Know Not Christ}
\lett{A}{lmighty} God, our heavenly Father, who in thy goodness hast caused the light of the Gospel to shine in our land: Extend thy mercy, we beseech thee, to the nations of the world that still walk in darkness; enlighten the Moslems with the knowledge of thy truth; and grant that the Gospel of salvation may be made known in all lands, that the heart of the peoples may be turned unto thee; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{Sick Persons}
\lett{A}{lmighty} Father, giver of life and health: Look mercifully, we beseech thee, on the sick and suffering, especially those for whom our prayers are desired; that, by thy blessing upon them and upon those who minister to them, they may be restored if it be thy gracious will, to health of body and mind, and give thanks to thee in thy holy Church; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Person about to Undergo an Operation}

Hold thou \changeable{him/her} up and \changeable{he/she} shall be safe; enable \changeable{him/her} to feel that thou art near, and to know that underneath are the everlasting arms; grant that resting on thy protection, \changeable{he/she} may fear no evil, since thou art with \changeable{him/her} and will comfort \changeable{him/her}; through Jesus Christ our Saviour.

\newcollect{Persons in Mental Darkness}
\lett{O}{ Heavenly} Father, we beseech thee to have mercy upon all thy children who are living in mental darkness. Restore them to strength of mind and cheerfulness of spirit, and give them health and peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Person Mentally Disturbed}
\lett{O}{ Heavenly} Father, who in thy love and wisdom knowest the anxieties and fears of thy children; whose Son Jesus Christ said to his disciples, It is I, be not afraid; and to the tempest, Peace, be still: Grant that thy servant \changeable{N.} may be strengthened to cast all \changeable{his/her} care upon thee, for thou carest for \changeable{him/her}. Give \changeable{him/her} quietness; give \changeable{him/her} unshaken trust; and may the day-spring from on high guide \changeable{his/her} feet into the way of peace; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Despondent Person}
\lett{C}{omfort}, we beseech thee, most gracious God, this thy servant, cast down and faint of heart amidst the sorrows and difficulties of the world; and grant that, by the power of thy Holy Spirit, \changeable{he/she} may be enabled to go upon \changeable{his/her} way rejoicing, and give thee continual thanks for thy sustaining providence; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
\inlineresp{Amen.}

\newcollect{A Person Needing Sleep}
\lett{O}{ Heavenly} Father, who givest thy children sleep for the refreshing of their souls and bodies: Grant this gift to thy servant \changeable{N.}; keep \changeable{him/her} in that perfect peace which thou hast promised to those whose minds are stayed on thee; inspire \changeable{him/her} with a sense of thy presence; so that in the hours of silence \changeable{he/she} may enjoy the blessed assurance of thy love; through Jesus Christ our Saviour.
\inlineresp{Amen.}
