\bcpchapter{Holy Communion}

\subsection{The Preparation}

\begin{rubric}
At the entry of the choir, servers and clergy, the congregation stands.
\end{rubric}

\subsection{The Introduction}

\begin{rubric}
On special occasions there may be a procession.\\
Before the procession the celebrant sings:\\
\blackrubric{Let us proceed in peace}\\
The choir and congregation reply:\\
\cross \blackrubric{In the name of Christ. Amen}

A hymn and/or an Introit may be sung.
\end{rubric}

\subsection{The Collect for Purity}

\noindent
\lett{A}{lmighty} God, unto whom all hearts be open, all desires known, and from whom no secrets are hid: Cleanse the thoughts of our hearts by the inspiration of thy Holy Spirit, that we may perfectly love thee, and worthily magnify thy holy name; through Christ our Lord. Amen.

\begin{rubric}
At a said Mass, the priest reads the Introit verse here.
\end{rubric}

\subsection{Gloria in Excelsis}

\begin{rubric}
This hymn of praise is omitted in Masses of Lent \& Advent, and on weekdays, except for holy days.\\
It may be sung by the choir in Latin on certain occasions.
\end{rubric}


\noindent
Glory be to God on high,\\
And in earth peace, goodwill towards men.\\
We praise thee, we bless thee,\\
we worship thee, we glorify thee,\\
we give thanks to thee for thy great glory,\\
O Lord God, heavenly King,\\
God the Father Almighty.

\noindent
O Lord, the only begotten son Jesu Christ;\\
O Lord God, Lamb of God,\\
Son of the Father,\\
That takest away the sins of the world, have mercy upon us.\\
Thou that takest away the sins of the world, have mercy upon us.\\
Thou that takest away the sins of the world, recieve our prayer.\\
Thou that sittest at the right hand of God the Father,\\
have mercy upon us.

\noindent
For thou only art holy,\\
thou only art the Lord;\\
Thou only, O Christ, with the Holy Ghost, art most high,\\
\cross in the glory of God the Father.\\
Amen.
